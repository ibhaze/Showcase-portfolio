ABOUT PROJECT

This project focuses on creating a toy exchange application where parents and children can swap, donate, or request toys easily within their community. The app promotes sustainability and affordability by encouraging families to reuse toys instead of constantly buying new ones.

Users can upload pictures and descriptions of toys they no longer need, browse toys from others nearby, and arrange safe exchanges or donations. The goal is to make playtime both eco-friendly and accessible for all children.

MAIN PROBLEM

Many households accumulate a large number of toys that are no longer used as children grow or lose interest. These toys often end up stored away, thrown out, or wasted, despite being in good condition. Meanwhile, other families struggle to afford new toys, limiting their children’s opportunities for play and development.

This imbalance creates waste, inequality, and environmental harm. There’s a clear need for a sustainable solution that connects families to share and reuse toys in a simple, trusted, and local way.

END RESULTS

By creating a simple and accessible way for parents to exchange toys, the app supports a culture of sharing that benefits both the community and the environment. Beyond reducing waste and unnecessary spending,

it opens the door for new features such as scheduling playdates, allowing families to extend their interactions beyond the app and strengthen local relationships.

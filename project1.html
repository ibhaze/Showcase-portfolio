ABOUT PROJECT

FloryBele is an app designed to help students care for their plants with ease. It provides detailed insights into their plants' specific needs, helping them keep their greenery alive and thriving.

Users can scan their plants with their phone's camera, and the app will offer detailed guidance on moisture requirements, sunlight exposure, soil quality, and optimal room temperature.
